01 The fjord

One day on the Nærøyfjord

06:40

First light

Mist sits on the water until the sun clears the eastern wall. Then it lifts, slowly, in sheets. Nothing here is in a hurry.

13:00

Clear

By midday the walls stand sharp and green. The fjord narrows to 250 metres and the rock rises more than 1,700 metres straight out of the water.

18:10

Alpenglow

The light goes copper on the high snow, then blue. One by one the cabin windows come on, and the water holds every one of them.

23:40

After dark

From October to March, on a clear night, the sky opens. Green first, then violet. You watch from the sauna steps, or from bed, with the window doing the work.

03 The seasons

The fjord keeps four calendars.

Light that doesn’t leave.

04 Things to do

Four slow ways to spend a day.

Nothing is booked for you and nothing is compulsory. These are the things guests tend to do, in roughly the order they do them.

Up to the ridge

Six waymarked paths start at the cabin doors. The ridge route is 5.5 kilometres with 620 metres of climbing, about three and a half hours, and ends with a view down the whole fjord. You are back in time to light the sauna.

  • 5.5 km
  • 620 m up
  • 3½ hours

The narrows, by kayak

A kayak waits at every jetty, with a vest and a dry bag. The narrows are 250 metres wide, and at dawn you hear the waterfalls before you see them. Guided paddles leave on Tuesdays and Fridays.

  • 2 hours
  • All levels
  • Included

Heat, then the fjord

Every cabin has its own wood-fired sauna at around 80 degrees. Step out, walk down, go in. The fjord sits between 4 and 14 degrees depending on the month. Three rounds is the local rule.

  • 80 °C
  • 4–14 °C water
  • Every cabin

The slow boat

The passenger ferry takes two hours and a quarter through the fjord and its narrowest arm. It leaves the Gudvangen quay at 10:15. We drive you down, and you come back on the 15:40.

  • 2 h 15
  • 10:15 from the quay
  • Transfer available

07 Questions

Before you come.

Something missing? Write to hei@hytta.example and a person will answer within a day.

There is no Wi-Fi, and in most cabins very little mobile signal. A landline in the boathouse handles emergencies, and every cabin has a printed map of the fjord. If you need to work, this is the wrong place, and we will say so.

Most guests drive to Gudvangen, about two and a half hours from Bergen, and park at the quay. A small boat takes you and your bags along the shore to your cabin. Exact times arrive with your confirmation.

Linen, towels, firewood for the stove and the sauna, kayaks, life vests, and a welcome basket with bread, butter and coffee. Breakfast baskets and the quay transfer are optional and priced in the availability form above. There is a one-off cleaning fee of 650 NOK.

From October to March. Clear sky matters more than the month. December to February gives the darkest nights, while October and March are milder and often very good. We cannot promise the lights, and we will not pretend to.

Children are welcome in Fonn and Bjørk. We provide life vests in children’s sizes and a short sauna rule sheet. Dogs are welcome in Bjørk only, on request, because sheep graze on the nearby path.

Free cancellation up to 30 days before arrival. After that the first night is not refunded, and the rest is returned up to 7 days before. We would rather move your dates than lose them, so please ask.
